1. Session 3 · History that cannot see the label

Architecture. Feature construction is where leakage would enter. Item rates and the shopper's past mix are allowed because a returning shopper and a known item have that history. The row being scored does not contribute its own fit label. Length, quality, and review text are not in the feature list at all.

2. Session 3 · Choose by log loss, report the size loss

Architecture. Training fits three models on the same matrix, calibrates the learners on the validation split, and picks the lowest validation log loss. The test split is scored after that choice. A separate size search asks which nearby size has the highest probability of fit. That search is allowed to lose to the most-common-fit-size rule, and the model card records the loss.

1:46 · Andrew · src/fit_finder/model/train.py on main

How this session connects

split.py holds out a shopper's later review. features.py builds history from training rows only, leave-one-out on the training table. train.py fits three models, calibrates the learners on validation, and chooses by validation log loss. Test is scored once.
