1. Session 4 · Rank inside the category

Architecture. The ranker is a content score over the item catalog, not a second model. It stays in the requested category. The score mixes historical fit rate, closeness of the usual fit size, and a small support term so a one-review fluke cannot win. Price and color are not observed on UCSD items, and the function says so instead of inventing them.

2. Session 4 · Hit rate on held-out fit reviews

Architecture. The offline metric asks a retrieval question. Take a held-out review that said the item fit. Hide that item among random other items in the same category. Did the ranker put it in the top ten? This is not a shopper experiment and it does not use the simulated arms.

1:13 · Andrew · src/fit_finder/recommend/evaluate.py on main

How this session connects

build_catalog rolls reviews up to one row per item. rank_items stays inside the category and scores fit rate, size closeness, and support. evaluate.py asks whether a held-out fit review's item lands in the top 10 of a same-category candidate set.
